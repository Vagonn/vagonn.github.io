Who we are

This policy applies to the Android game Bowling Master (package name com.fatalbug.bowling), published on Google Play by the developer FatalBug ("we", "us"). Contact: support.fatalbug@gmail.com.

Data we access, collect, use and share

None. The game runs entirely on your device and has no internet permission, so it cannot send any information off your device. We do not access, collect, use or share personal or sensitive user data, including contacts, location, device identifiers, photos, files or account information. No third-party libraries or SDKs that collect data are included.

Data stored on your device

The game may keep simple game settings (such as sound on/off) in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Security

Because no user data is transmitted or stored outside your device, there is no data held by us that could be exposed. Any local game settings are kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox.

Retention and deletion

We retain no user data. Local game settings are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
